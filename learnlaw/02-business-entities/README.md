# Module 02: Business entities

The LLC and its relatives. How to choose a form, build a Florida LLC that holds up, govern it, keep the shield, and get out. The Florida Revised Limited Liability Company Act, ch. 605, is the spine; ch. 607 (corporations) and ch. 620 (partnerships) are the comparisons.

| 2 | [Forming a Florida LLC](02-forming-a-florida-llc.md) | Walk the Sunbiz steps from name to annual report, with the fees, and know which habits actually keep the shield up. |
| 3 | [Operating agreements](03-operating-agreements.md) | Say what ch. 605 decides when the agreement is silent, what § 605.0105 will not let you change, and which clauses every agreement needs. |
