# Module 01: Foundations

The map of the court system and the machinery every later module runs on: which court, what power it has, how a case moves, and what a winner actually gets.

| # | Lesson | What you will be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Federal and state courts](01-federal-and-state-courts.md) | Name the court a Florida business dispute would land in, from county court to the Florida Supreme Court and from the Middle District to the Eleventh Circuit, and say why a lawyer might prefer one system over the other. |
| 2 | [Jurisdiction and venue](02-jurisdiction-and-venue.md) | Work through personal jurisdiction under *International Shoe*, *Burger King*, and § 48.193; subject-matter jurisdiction including LLC citizenship for diversity; and venue under § 47.011. |
| 3 | [Civil procedure in outline](03-civil-procedure-in-outline.md) | Follow a case from complaint to appeal, know the deadlines that cannot be missed, and recognize the waiver traps: affirmative defenses, compulsory counterclaims, and the 2021 summary judgment standard. |
| 4 | [Remedies](04-remedies.md) | Price a claim: compensatory and consequential damages, liquidated damages, punitive damages under §§ 768.72 and 768.73, injunctions, specific performance, rescission, declaratory relief, fees, and interest. |

Cases this module relies on: [`marbury-v-madison`](../cases/marbury-v-madison.md), [`erie-v-tompkins`](../cases/erie-v-tompkins.md), [`international-shoe-v-washington`](../cases/international-shoe-v-washington.md), [`burger-king-v-rudzewicz`](../cases/burger-king-v-rudzewicz.md), [`hadley-v-baxendale`](../cases/hadley-v-baxendale.md).

Then do [Drill 04, the unpaid invoice](../09-drills/04-the-unpaid-invoice.md), which exercises jurisdiction, procedure, and remedies together.
