# Module 09: Drills

Business scenarios with model issue-spotting answers. Each one is worked with the seven steps from [the issue-spotting method](../00-how-law-works/05-issue-spotting-method.md): parties, timeline, walk the doctrines, flip it, remedies and procedure, what you do not know, rank and name the red herrings.

Write your answer in full before opening the model answer. The model answers are not the only right answers. If you spotted an issue the model missed, write it down and check it against the lesson.

## Suggested order

1. **Drill 00** right after module 00. It is deliberately simple and the model answer spells out each step.
2. **Drill 04** after module 03 and **Drill 01** after module 02. These two are the bread and butter of small-business disputes: a customer who will not pay and a co-owner who will not share.
3. **Drill 03** after module 04 and **Drill 02** after module 05.
4. **Drill 07** after module 06, then **Drills 05 and 06** after module 07.
5. **Drill 08** after module 08. Budget two hours. Then go back to Drill 00 and do it again; the difference between your two answers is what you learned.

## How to grade yourself

For each issue in the model answer, mark whether you (a) spotted it, (b) named the governing rule, (c) saw the other side's defense or counterclaim, and (d) wrote down the facts you would need. Most people spot the claims early and miss the defenses, the procedure, and the open questions. That is what the drills train.
