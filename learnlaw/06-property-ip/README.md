# Module 06: Property and intellectual property

The space you rent and the things you own that nobody can touch: your name, your content, your secrets, your inventions. The lease lesson is Florida landlord-tenant law for nonresidential premises. The IP lessons are mostly federal, with Florida's trademark and trade secret statutes layered on. The last lesson is the one most owners learn too late: who actually owns the logo, the code, and the content you paid someone else to make.

| # | Lesson | What you will be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Commercial leases](01-commercial-leases.md) | Read a Florida nonresidential lease clause by clause, know what chapter 83 gives each side, and see where a personal guaranty bites. |
| 2 | [Trademarks](02-trademarks.md) | Tell a protectable mark from a weak one, run a clearance search, and choose between federal, Florida, and no registration. |
| 3 | [Copyright](03-copyright.md) | Know what is protected the moment it is created, why registration still matters, and how fair use and the DMCA work. |
| 4 | [Trade secrets](04-trade-secrets.md) | Identify what qualifies under Florida's UTSA and the federal DTSA, and what "reasonable measures" means in practice. |
| 5 | [Patents in outline](05-patents-in-outline.md) | Understand the basic requirements, the one-year clock, provisional applications, and when a patent is not worth pursuing. |
| 6 | [IP ownership: work for hire and contractors](06-ip-ownership-work-for-hire-and-contractors.md) | Say who owns what an employee or contractor creates, and fix it with an assignment. |
