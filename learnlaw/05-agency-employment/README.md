# Module 05: Agency and employment

Who can bind your business, when you answer for what other people do, and the rules that govern the people you pay. Florida is an at-will state with a strong non-compete statute and a constitutional minimum wage, and almost every lesson here has both a federal and a Florida layer.

| # | Lesson | What you will be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Agency and authority](01-agency-and-authority.md) | Say when a member, manager, or employee has bound the LLC, and sign so that you do not bind yourself. |
| 2 | [Vicarious liability](02-vicarious-liability.md) | Work out when the business pays for an employee's or contractor's tort, including Florida's dangerous instrumentality doctrine. |
| 3 | [At-will employment](03-at-will-employment.md) | Explain what at-will does and does not let you do, and name the statutory exceptions that generate lawsuits. |
| 4 | [Employee vs contractor](04-employee-vs-contractor.md) | Apply the right-to-control, IRS, DOL, and workers' comp tests and price the cost of getting it wrong. |
| 5 | [Wage and hour](05-wage-and-hour.md) | Apply the FLSA and Florida minimum wage rules, overtime, exemptions, and the Florida pre-suit notice. |
| 6 | [Non-competes and restrictive covenants](06-non-competes-and-restrictive-covenants.md) | Read § 542.335 and the 2025 CHOICE Act the way a Florida judge does, and choose the lightest covenant that works. |
| 7 | [Discrimination and retaliation](07-discrimination-and-retaliation.md) | Spot the protected classes, the employee-count thresholds, the charge deadlines, and the burden-shifting framework. |
| 8 | [Hiring and firing compliance](08-hiring-and-firing-compliance.md) | Run a hire and a separation through the federal and Florida checklist without creating a claim. |
