# Module 08: Disputes and procedure

What happens when a dispute becomes a case. The sequence of a lawsuit, the small claims shortcut, the letters and notices that must come first, the fee-shifting rules that change what a case is worth, the alternatives to trial, the tools for turning a judgment into money, the doctrines that keep assets out of reach, and what to do in the first three weeks after being served.

| 2 | [Small claims and county court](02-small-claims-and-county-court.md) | Decide whether a dispute belongs in small claims, who can appear for the business, and what a counterclaim does to the forum. |
