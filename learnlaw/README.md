# learnlaw

A self-paced, case-driven course in how law works, built for a business owner in Florida.

Scope: **United States federal law and Florida law**, with a business and LLC focus. It teaches doctrines, walks through landmark cases, shows how courts actually reason, and drills the habit of spotting every angle in a situation.

It is education, not advice. Nothing here tells you what to do about your specific situation. It teaches you to understand the rules, read the sources, and ask a lawyer sharper questions.

## How to work through it

1. **Do module 00 first, in order.** It is the toolkit. Every later lesson assumes you can brief a case and spot issues the way module 00 teaches.
2. **Read the lesson, then the linked cases.** Do not skip the cases. The rule is the summary; the case is where you see the reasoning and the fights over the edges.
3. **Do the drill at the end of each lesson** before reading the model answer. Write your answer down. The habit is the product.
4. **Fill in the progress tracker** as you go.
5. **Verify anything you plan to rely on.** Every lesson cites the primary source (statute section or case). Florida changes its statutes every session. Check the current text at Online Sunshine before you act on a number or a deadline.

## Rules of the road for this course

- **Jurisdiction tags.** Every lesson is tagged `[US]` for federal or general American common law, `[FL]` for Florida. Where Florida differs from the majority rule, the lesson says so.
- **Numbers carry a date.** Fees, thresholds, limitation periods, and wage figures are stated as of October 2026 and flagged "verify" because they move.
- **Cases are anchors, not authority for your situation.** A case shows how a court reasoned on its facts. Your facts differ. That is the whole point of the issue-spotting method.
- **"It depends" is a real answer.** The course teaches you what it depends on.

## Where to find primary sources

- Florida Statutes: Online Sunshine, `leg.state.fl.us`
- Florida entity filings: Sunbiz, `dos.fl.gov/sunbiz`
- Florida court opinions: Florida Supreme Court and District Courts of Appeal websites, Google Scholar, CourtListener
- Federal statutes: U.S. Code at `uscode.house.gov`
- Federal cases: Supreme Court at `supremecourt.gov`, Eleventh Circuit at `ca11.uscourts.gov`, Google Scholar, CourtListener
- Florida Rules of Civil Procedure and Small Claims Rules: The Florida Bar website
