# Module 04: Torts

A tort is a civil wrong that does not depend on a contract. For a business, torts are the claims that come from customers who get hurt, from competitors, from people who were lied to, and from people who say false things. This module covers the torts a Florida business is most likely to face or bring, how Florida courts analyze duty and fault, and the 2023 reforms that changed the math.
