# Module 07: Regulatory compliance

The rules that apply to a business because of what it does, not because anyone sued it. Licenses, taxes, consumer protection, data, marketing, raising money, and federal paperwork. Each lesson teaches the regime's trigger, its elements, how courts and agencies read the edges, and what the penalty actually is.

| # | Lesson | What you will be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Licensing and registrations](01-licensing-and-registrations.md) | Walk the stack of state, local, tax, and name registrations, and explain why an unlicensed contractor's contract is unenforceable under § 489.128. |
| 2 | [Florida taxes for business](02-florida-taxes-for-business.md) | Separate sales and use tax, reemployment tax, corporate tax, tangible property tax, and stamp tax, and see where personal liability attaches. |
| 3 | [FDUTPA](03-fdutpa.md) | State the elements of a private FDUTPA claim, its actual-damages ceiling, and its two-way fee rule, and spot the per se hooks. |
| 4 | [Data privacy and breach](04-data-privacy-and-breach.md) | Apply § 501.171's notice clock and know which federal regime and which contract terms also apply. |
| 5 | [Marketing, telemarketing, and advertising](05-marketing-telemarketing-advertising.md) | Check an ad, an email, a text campaign, a review program, or a sweepstakes against FTC rules, the TCPA, and the Florida Telephone Solicitation Act. |
| 6 | [Raising money: securities basics](06-raising-money-securities-basics.md) | Apply *Howey* to an LLC interest or note, name the exemption being used, and explain rescission exposure. |
| 7 | [Federal reporting and miscellaneous compliance](07-federal-reporting-and-misc.md) | Keep the checklist: EIN, 1099s, I-9 and E-Verify, OSHA, ADA, and the current state of beneficial ownership reporting. |
