# Module 03: Contracts

Contracts are the operating system of a business. This module covers how they form, what they mean, when they break, what a breach is worth, and the special rules for goods and online deals. Each lesson teaches the general American rule, how courts reason at the edges, and where Florida follows or departs.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Formation: offer, acceptance, and the objective theory](01-formation.md) | Tell when an email thread became a contract, and when a counteroffer killed the deal. |
| 2 | [Consideration and promissory estoppel](02-consideration-and-promissory-estoppel.md) | Spot a promise that is not enforceable, and know when Florida will enforce it anyway. |
| 3 | [The statute of frauds and what counts as a writing](03-statute-of-frauds-and-writings.md) | Know which deals need a signed writing, what counts as one, and how e-signatures fit. |

| 5 | [Performance, conditions, and breach](05-performance-conditions-breach.md) | Decide whether a breach lets you stop performing, and avoid becoming the breaching party. |
| 6 | [Defenses to enforcement](06-defenses.md) | Recognize mistake, fraud, duress, unconscionability, illegality, and impossibility when they show up. |
| 7 | [Contract remedies](07-remedies.md) | Size a claim: expectation, reliance, foreseeability, mitigation, liquidated damages, fees. |
