# Module 00: How law works

Start here. This module is the toolkit the rest of the course uses. Work through it in order.

| # | Lesson | What you will be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Sources of law and the hierarchy of authority](01-sources-and-hierarchy.md) | Say what kind of law governs a question, which court's word is final, and where to look it up. |
| 2 | [How to read a case](02-how-to-read-a-case.md) | Brief any opinion: facts, issue, holding, reasoning, and what it does and does not decide. |
| 3 | [How to read a statute](03-how-to-read-a-statute.md) | Take apart a Florida Statutes section, find its definitions, and read it the way a court will. |
| 4 | [How courts reason](04-how-courts-reason.md) | Recognize precedent, analogy, standards vs rules, burdens of proof, and policy arguments when you see them. |
| 5 | [The issue-spotting method](05-issue-spotting-method.md) | Turn a messy business situation into a list of claims, defenses, and open questions, from every party's side. |

Then do [Drill 00](../09-drills/00-first-drill.md) before moving on.
